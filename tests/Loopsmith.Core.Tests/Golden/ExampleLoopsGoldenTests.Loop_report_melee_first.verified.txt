Steps
  Open with the melee instead of the dodge: slide-melee to jolt the pack, then skip grenade and
  Arc weapon into it, grab the orbs and dodge last to refill the melee. Gambler's Dodge's melee
  refill depends on the Melee stat, so the rules give it as unknown ("?").
  1. pack:new             The next group of enemies: a new pack, nothing on it yet
  2. melee:kill           Combination Blow: Tempest Strike jolts, Impact Induction refunds grenade energy
  3. max:bolt-charge      Bolt Charge at x10 — Shinobu's Vow and Flashover; the next ability hit discharges it
  4. grenade:kill         Skip grenade into the jolted pack: its hit discharges Bolt Charge; Amplified and Ionic Traces
  5. energy:kill          Arc weapon kill: Spark of Discharge traces, Harmonic Siphon orb
  6. pickup:orb-of-power  Orbs: Armor Charge and Unraveling Rounds
  7. class                Gambler's Dodge to refill the melee (the amount depends on the Melee stat)

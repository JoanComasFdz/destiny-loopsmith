Steps
  The creator's loop: dodge to arm Slice and Reaper, skip grenade into the pack, clean up with
  the Strand and Arc weapons, grab the orbs, skip grenade again. Every jolted kill feeds Bolt
  Charge and Ionic Traces, which give grenade energy back. Tempest Strike's Bolt Charge on those
  kills doesn't stack with Dielectric's, so the analysis lists it as wasted.
  1. class                Gambler's Dodge: arms Slice and Reaper; Bomber refunds grenade energy
  2. grenade:kill         Skip grenade into the pack: Spark of Shock jolts, the jolted kill gives Amplified, Bolt Charge and an Ionic Trace
  3. kinetic:kill         Festival Flight: Slice severs, Reaper and Attrition Orbs drop orbs, To Shreds gives Woven Mail (and unravels once the pack is severed)
  4. energy:kill          Arc weapon on the jolted pack: Spark of Discharge traces, Harmonic Siphon orb
  5. pickup:orb-of-power  Grab the orbs: Armor Charge for Grenade Kickstart, and Unraveling Rounds
  6. grenade:kill         Second skip grenade: Kickstart spends Armor Charge; max Bolt Charge strikes and refunds the grenade

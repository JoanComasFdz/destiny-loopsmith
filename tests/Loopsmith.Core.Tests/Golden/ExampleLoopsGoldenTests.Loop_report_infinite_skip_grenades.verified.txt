Steps
  The creator's loop: dodge to arm Slice and Reaper, skip grenade into the pack, clean up with
  the Strand and Arc weapons, grab the orbs, skip grenade again. Every jolted kill feeds Bolt
  Charge and Ionic Traces back into grenade energy: the rules refund more than the two grenades
  a cycle throws (ability energy isn't simulated, so the analysis adds the refunds up).
  1. class                Gambler's Dodge: arms Slice and Reaper; Bomber refunds grenade energy
  2. grenade:kill         Skip grenade into the pack: Spark of Shock jolts, the jolted kill gives Amplified, Bolt Charge and Ionic Traces
  3. kinetic:kill         Festival Flight: Slice severs, Reaper and Attrition Orbs drop orbs, To Shreds unravels
  4. energy:kill          Arc weapon on the jolted pack: Spark of Discharge traces, Harmonic Siphon and Dielectric orbs
  5. pickup:orb-of-power  Grab the orbs: Grenade Kickstart's Armor Charge and Unraveling Rounds
  6. grenade:kill         Second skip grenade: Kickstart spends Armor Charge; max Bolt Charge strikes and refunds the grenade

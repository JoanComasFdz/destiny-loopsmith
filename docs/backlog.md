# Backlog

Open items. What Loopsmith is and how it behaves is in [requirements.md](requirements.md),
[ADRs.md](ADRs.md), [rule-format.md](rule-format.md) and [loop-format.md](loop-format.md); this
list is what the code doesn't do yet, what the format can't express yet, and what is still to decide.

## Data

- **Compendium parser** — the 2026-10-09 snapshot was ingested by hand into the Skip Grenade build's
  rules (`compendium/2026-10-09/<tab>#<row>` provenance; source disagreements in
  [builds/skip-grenade-hunter/discrepancies.md](../builds/skip-grenade-hunter/discrepancies.md)). Next:
  a parser slice (a registry of per-tab parsers) instead of hand copying, and the coverage report
  (requirements FR-11). To refresh, run `tools/compendium/get-compendium.ps1` (Windows) or `.sh` locally
  and hand the zip to a session, or let the session run `sheet_dump.py` itself
  ([tools/compendium/README.md](../tools/compendium/README.md)).
  The raw snapshot stays private (`snapshots/` is gitignored).
- **Secondary abilities of other subclasses** — the engine takes them (D9), the rules author only the Arc
  Hunter's (Tempest Strike's `melee:slide`, Ascension's `class:air`). Open, from the Compendium: the grenade
  held (Chaos Accelerant, Bleak Watcher, Mindspun Invocation, Unbreakable), the melee while sliding
  (Lightning Surge, Consecration, Howl of the Storm, Flechette Storm) and in the air (Ballistic Slam).
- **Clarity ingestion slice** — Clarity is reachable (git clone): `descriptions/clarity.json` maps a
  hash to its name, type and descriptions. Its numbers are in the rules by hand (v2.0625).
- **Bungie manifest** (FR-13) — `rules/manifest.yaml` is an excerpt for the hashes Loopsmith names,
  regenerated by hand (`tools/manifest/`) when the rules or a saved DIM share gain a hash, or Bungie
  publishes a new manifest (noticing that is open). The web app reads Bungie's full manifest in the browser
  for what the excerpt lacks (a pasted link's other items; `ManifestJoining`). Open:
  - **One reading of the manifest:** the Python extractor and the C# join say the same thing twice (a test
    checks it against a downloaded manifest, `LOOPSMITH_MANIFEST_DIR`). The CLI could write the excerpt with
    the join and retire the Python.
  - **The CLI** reads only the excerpt; a build there with other items shows them by hash.
  - **The pinned content id** (`ManifestContentId`, used when the manifest index doesn't answer) has to be
    updated with the excerpt; Bungie may drop old component files.
  - **Phones:** the item table is ~9 MB to download and ~215 MB in memory, held for as long as the tab is open (as
    DIM holds its copy), and the join's rounds still hold the page for up to ~1 s each on a desktop (the last round,
    which builds the excerpt, the longest): a phone feels them. Splitting the excerpt's build from the last round, or
    asking for fewer fields of each item, would shorten them; keeping only the fields Loopsmith reads (as DIM trims
    its tables) would hold less.
  - Hashes the excerpt can't give by a unique name: Horde Shuttle (two artifact perks of that name).

## Rule format gaps (what the Skip Grenade build couldn't express)

- **The rest of the Arc Hunter** (`rules/hunter/arc.yaml`: every Arcstrider plug is authored, compendium/2026-10-13).
  What its elements couldn't express, so they are `inert` or fire on more than they should:
  - steps that don't exist: taking damage at critical health (Spark of Instinct's jolting burst — the only one that
    would change a loop: its Jolt feeds Spark of Ions and Lethal Current), being hit by a melee
    attack (Spark of Feedback), sprinting (Spark of Haste and Focus: no loop would change, as with a jump), sliding over
    an ammo brick (Spark of Momentum's x3 Bolt Charge). Reaching critical health is a step the player declares
    (`health:critical`, Emergency Reinforcement, #69); the next task builds on it:
    **The critical-health Sparks react to "Critical health".** Both are `inert` today, and Spark of Recharge's reason
    ("Critical health is no step") is out of date since #69.
    1. **Spark of Recharge** (Arc#22): on reaching critical health, grenade and melee regenerate 400% [PvP 200%] faster
       until your shields are full. Its faster regeneration shows as an outcome on the "Critical health" step (the
       owner: it belongs in the step even though it sets nothing else off), so it is no longer `inert`; check against
       the Compendium that it is ability regeneration, not health.
    2. **Spark of Instinct** (Arc#18): taking damage *while* at critical health sends out an Arc burst that jolts nearby
       enemies (its Jolt feeds Spark of Ions and Lethal Current). Decide how the player declares "taking damage at
       critical health" (D3): critical health leaves nothing in the state today.
    Use the Compendium snapshot for every value (`"?"` where it has none), record disagreements in the build's
    `discrepancies.md`, and look for other authored elements that react to critical health;
  - a weapon's ammo type (Spark of Beacons: Special and Heavy Arc kills only — a `chance` rule on every Arc weapon
    kill) and precision kills (Spark of Brilliance — `chance`);
  - kills within a time of each other (Spark of Amplitude's 2 kills within 4 s — `chance`) and a rule's cooldown
    (Spark of Ions' 10 s between traces: it fires on every jolted kill);
  - Storm's Edge's teleport to its knife, Lethal Current's longer lunge: movement, which has no part in a loop.
- **Triggers:** champion stun (Defibrillating Blast's "stun → max Bolt Charge", with an outcome that
  puts a buff at max); an air move that spends another ability's energy (Weavewalk's 20% melee energy;
  secondary uses are the abilities' own — `melee:slide`, `melee:air`, `class:air`, `grenade:hold`, D9);
  blocking / reflecting attacks (Arc Staff's guard grants Bolt Charge per reflected attack).
- **Damage sources:** a specific ability or an ability's element (Skip Grenade vs any grenade, "Arc
  grenades"; Momentum Transfer doesn't work with Grapple), "this weapon" for weapon perks. ("A weapon of your
  Super's element" is `weapon:harmonic`.)
- **Conditions:** "target lacks a debuff"; "target has any of" (Dielectric counts Jolt only, Photonic
  Flare Sever only); "while a kind of mod is equipped" (an Orb of Power gives Armor Charge only with an
  Armor Charge mod equipped); "while in your super" (Arc Staff's damage resistance).
- **Declared states:** a level below the max (`stacks:<status>:<n>`, for "Combination Blow ×2"); the
  target's tier (D7: the pack is `minor`, so a kill rule for a higher tier never fires in a loop — "punch
  the big enemy"); "repeat until <state>" as a marker on a step.
- **Facts the format can't show yet** (shown, never applied — most of this build's `?` would become
  readable): health in HP (`restoreHealth` reads as a percentage, so Combination Blow 100 → 40 HP,
  Defibrillating Blast ~55 HP, To Shreds 15 HP per pulse stay `?`); values by declared level (Combination
  Blow +133% / +266% / +400% melee damage); amounts that scale with a stat (Gambler's Dodge: 1% melee per
  Melee point); an orb's energy by what spawned it; ability regeneration and damage resistance (Flow
  State, Tempest Strike, Amplified's extras); an "all weapons" archetype. One "described fact" value and
  passive would cover them. The ability profiles (`charges`, `chunkScalar`, `baseCooldown`) are recorded
  but shown nowhere: show them in `explain`.
- **Picking armor mods** (the designer's sockets and mod picker, [loop-format.md](loop-format.md#armor-mods), D30):
  a tuning mod (a Tier 5 copy's tuning socket) can't be picked, only kept; a stat mod the manifest excerpt's
  `statPlugs` lacks (the artifact's cheaper Grenade Mod) doesn't add to the stats; the build check doesn't warn about a
  build already over a piece's energy (a DIM loadout's), only a pick is refused; a mod in an artifice socket shows the
  artifice mods only (the game takes a general one there too).
- **Armor mods** (all current ones of the five slots are in `rules/mods/`; raid, activity and seasonal mods aren't
  supported, by decision: no picker offers them and a DIM link's are ignored, ADRs D35 — supporting them, if asked
  for, takes declarations for an encounter's mechanics: the pack's race and tier, a relic carried, Flux of Darkness,
  a plate stood on): no trigger for the damage you take (critical health is a step the player declares,
  `health:critical`; breaking a shield and an ability's last charge are options of a step, `:shield` and `:last`, D36);
  an ability's energy isn't tracked, so with more than one charge the player marks the use that spends the last one (a
  later step: a use empties it, each energy grant fills it, and the last charge follows from the loop); Benevolent Finisher's x3 isn't counted off (it
  is inert: allies only); an overshield's HP
  (Proximity Ward); two mods that can't both pay out on different events (Hands-On and Ashes to Assets); a cooldown
  between orbs (Firepower on `grenade:kill:3` drops three). Ammo, weapon handling, damage taken and allies-only mods are
  inert by design (D1). Two of them are tasks of their own, next:
  - **Dynamo's Super energy by class** (`rules/mods/helmet.yaml`): 2.5% | 4% with 1 | 2 mods, times the class's
    multiplier (Hunter x1, Titan x1.2, Warlock x2; Compendium Armor Mods#5). A rule can't read the build's class, so
    the amount is `?`; a value by class would show the build's.
  - **The order the mods are slotted in**: Firepower and Heavy Handed both drop their orb only when Firepower sits
    before Heavy Handed (left to right, `rules/mods/arms.yaml`). No rule reads the order of the mods, so both always
    fire; the order is only a fact in their descriptions.
- **Weapon mods** (every one the Compendium lists is in `rules/mods/weapons.yaml`; all inert but Synergy): the
  manifest's other weapon mods aren't authored — the old Adept mods (Adept Range, Adept Mag…), Enhanced Heat Generated
  and Vent Speed, Full Auto Retrofit, the bow and activity mods (Jumpshot, Tungsten Bowstring, Fikrul's Friends…) —
  so they show with their ⊘ and set nothing off. Synergy's cooldowns (the pickups' 0–12 s, its own) and the counter's
  other enemy tiers (elite 16.7%, boss 50%: the pack is rank-and-file, ADRs D7) are facts in its description. What
  picking up a Firesprite, Void Breach, Stasis Shard or Tangle gives has no keyword element yet.
- **Written as declarations, *(chance)* or facts** (D3, D8), so they need no new format: progress the
  game doesn't show as a stacking status (Spark of Discharge's kills per trace, Amplified's intrinsic kill counter)
  is *(chance)*; hits or damage kept up on one enemy (To Shreds' 6 weapon hits, Horde Shuttle's 10%, Attrition Orbs,
  Lightning Rounds' 10th hit) are `sustained`, sure on a hit step and not on a kill step (D34); Slice's 5 hits end with `max:slice`; energy per
  stack, refunds, chunk scalars and rule cooldowns (Impact Induction, Photonic Flare) are facts; Spark of
  Resistance's "3+ enemies" always holds.

## Engine

- **Prismatic** builds need per-ability elements; an ability takes the subclass's element.
- **One pack** (D7) is optimistic for a single boss; single-target nuance would be a refinement.
- **Rename the `Simulation` slice**: it plays steps and analyses a loop's order; it simulates nothing (D1).
- **A folded step shows the total** (PR #80's test plan, T4): a step's identical events fold into one block marked ×N,
  with each outcome once ("Crisis Inverted 💀 kill ×3" → "+10% grenade energy" for Demolitionist's three kills). The
  owner wants the total there, "+30% grenade energy", and the per-event amount in the why ("10% per kill, 3 kills").
  It changes every folded block (five folded "+1 Bolt Charge" would read "+5 Bolt Charge"), so it needs a line in
  ADRs D1, which today says a number is shown with its outcome, never added up.
- **Count the hits a source needs** (PR #80's test plan, T12; revisits D34): a hit step stands for damage kept up, so a
  `sustained` source fires on any hit step, even of one enemy (Thunderlord's Lightning Rounds strikes, and Return
  Stroke reloads, on "Thunderlord (🥊 hit)"). The owner wants the count: a source that needs N hits fires for sure on a
  hit step of at least N (the enemy-count selector's number, up to 20), and not below; a kill still strikes
  (Reign Havoc). The sources: Lightning Rounds every 10th hit (and Return Stroke with it), Attrition Orbs (a
  magazine-based count, 3 for a Grenade Launcher), To Shreds' 6 hits, Horde Shuttle's 10% of the enemy's health (`?`
  hits). ADRs D34 and the rules' `sustained` change with it.
- **One heading per weapon for a perk on two weapons** (PR #80's test plan, T3): with the enhanced Demolitionist on
  Astral Horizon and Crisis Inverted, a grenade step shows both reloads ("reloads your kinetic weapon", "reloads your
  energy weapon") under "WEAPON PERK · ASTRAL HORIZON"; Crisis Inverted's copy gets no heading of its own. Each weapon's
  copy of a perk is its own element (D31), so each should show under its own weapon: "WEAPON PERK · CRISIS INVERTED" /
  "Demolitionist (enhanced)" / "reloads your energy weapon".

## Web

- **An ability's charges in the HUD**: the game draws a small blue rectangle under the grenade's (melee's, class
  ability's) icon for each charge, so Shinobu's Vow's second grenade shows as two. The designer's HUD could draw one
  per charge the build gives the ability (its profile's `charges` plus every `extraCharges`, ADRs D36), next to the
  "⬜ Spend fully" box. Only how many the build has: an ability's energy isn't tracked, so none of them is shown empty.
- **First visit downloads ≈ 3 MB** (the .NET runtime as WebAssembly + the engine: 2.7 MB brotli,
  3.2 MB gzip in a Release publish; cached afterwards). The `wasm-tools` workload would shrink it;
  it can't be installed in cloud sessions, but the deploy workflows on GitHub's runners could
  install it.
- **No saved library.** The app stores nothing in the browser but Bungie's manifest (D25): a loop is kept by exporting its
  `.loop.yaml` or copying its share link, and loops imported on the Compare screen last until the tab
  closes. A library would need per-browser storage, or a backend to sync it across devices.
- **A build picker** over the catalog instead of build files (FR-1).
- **A weapon whose type isn't known is a parse error** (PR #80's test plan, T11): a build file's weapon with neither
  `hash` nor `archetype` parses, and a perk that goes by the weapon's archetype (Demolitionist's 10%, 20% on Shotguns)
  then shows both amounts. The designer never makes one (a picked weapon and a DIM link's take their type from Bungie's
  manifest), so a build file should be refused too: "weapon 'Any Arc weapon' needs an `archetype` or a `hash`". The
  repo's own builds give their placeholders one first (Skip Grenade Hunter's "Any Arc weapon"). A perk's details, on
  no weapon, still show both amounts.
- **A preview card for a pasted link**: Home draws a DIM build card for the shares it ships with; a pasted
  link that carries its loadout could show the same card before "Start designing".
- **Editing the rest of a build in the designer**: a weapon's perks can be picked (column by column, from
  the manifest excerpt's trait columns), the subclass edited (abilities, aspects and fragments, from the excerpt's
  `plugs`, for each of the nine Light subclasses) and any weapon or armor slot filled, swapped or emptied (every item of
  the game, or the player's own after a login), the mods picked and the artifact changed with its perks; changing the
  class or the subclass itself means a new DIM link or build file. The rules author only Arcstrider's elements, so
  another subclass's plugs show ⊘ and set nothing off until their rules are written.
- **A weapon's roll from Bungie**: a DIM link never carries one, so it is picked by hand; reading the
  equipped character's weapons by Bungie name (their sockets are public) needs a Bungie API key (owner).
- **Exotic weapons beyond Thunderlord**: only Thunderlord's perks and catalyst are authored
  (`rules/exotics/weapons.yaml`); other exotics show Bungie's text but set nothing off. Their rules use
  `slot:<slot>` for "this weapon".
- **What the rules can't say yet** (each noted in its rule file): stuns, "while holding the trigger". Ammo, reload
  speed and a weapon's own lightning strike are outside the loop on purpose (Feeding Frenzy is `inert`; Return Stroke's
  strike is Thunderlord's kill or hit step): Loopsmith isn't a simulator. A reload is a step the player declares, and an
  outcome (D37).
- **The artifact of a DIM link made since Monument of Triumph**: the excerpt has all seven artifacts, but a DIM link
  is read for its artifact perks only (`parameters.artifactUnlocks`), so its build's artifact is the one that unlocks
  the most of them and each perk is placed in a slot by Loopsmith (the highest tiers first, each in the free slot that
  takes the fewest tiers, [loop-format.md](loop-format.md#artifact)). Next: if DIM now saves the equipped artifact and
  the perk in each slot with a loadout, read them, so the designer opens a DIM loadout with the same artifact and the
  same perk in each slot (`artifact: { hash, slots }`); if DIM doesn't save them, say so and close this item.
- **Artifact perks' rules**: of the 147 perks of the seven artifacts (`rules/manifest.yaml`, `kind: artifact`) the rules
  author 7, the Tablet of Ruin's of the skip-grenade build (`rules/artifact/current.yaml`); the others show ⊘ in the
  artifact editor and set nothing off. Next: the rest of the Tablet of Ruin's 21, then the artifact to do after it,
  from the Compendium snapshot's Artifact Perks tab (`rule-author`; no invented numbers, D13).
- **Weapon perks**: the rules author 11 (Slice, Attrition Orbs, Feeding Frenzy, Demolitionist, and the ones that need
  several enemies in one action: One for All, Stats for All, Reaper's Tithe, Hatchling, Veteran's Wisdom, Unrelenting
  and Radiolaria Transposer), each with its enhanced trait (rule-format.md, "Enhanced traits"); the game has ~226
  enhanced traits and ~86 enhanced origin traits, whose base perks aren't authored either. A perk authored next gets
  its `enhanced` block (a test asks for it). Every perk says "this weapon" (`weapon:this`), bound to the weapon that
  rolls it; a perk reacts to a reload (`reload`) and reloads a weapon (`reloadWeapon`, D37). Next:
  - **Every weapon perk that feeds the loop.** With `weapon:this`, the reload trigger and the reload outcome, author
    every perk that gives or spends something a step needs (ability energy, a buff, a debuff, a pickup, an orb, a
    summon, or a reload another perk reacts to), e.g. Wellspring, Pugilist, Voltshot (kill, then reload: the next
    shots jolt), Incandescent, Destabilizing Rounds and Repulsor Brace, each with its enhanced trait; the
    full list is agreed with the owner first. A perk that only changes stats, handling, damage or ammo gets an
    `inert:` reason when it comes up, else stays ⊘. Sources: the Compendium snapshot and Clarity (`rule-author`,
    provenance on every rule, `"?"` for what they don't give); rerun `tools/manifest/` for the new hashes.

- **Stats, next** (D28): the stat facts the game gives with an outcome — an orb's heal by Health (0.7 HP per
  point, compendium/2026-10-09/Game Mechanics#119), a dodge's Overshield above 100 Class (#139), the damage
  bonuses above 100 (#126, #132, #138, #139) and Gambler's Dodge's refund as its number ("+79% melee energy") —
  need a rule value that reads a build stat; refunds below 70 of their stat scale down by an amount Bungie
  hasn't published ("?"). DIM's runtime stat benefits (Font mods while Armor Charge) aren't added up.
- **Damage resistance, next** (D29): only resistance passives count. Sources that are still only a description
  aren't counted: Speed Booster's 15% against combatants (sprinting isn't
  modelled), and the armor set bonuses' and artifact perks' (none of the authored ones has one; the Compendium lists
  about 15 set bonuses and 6 artifact perks with one, mostly a "Resist x1" buff on some trigger). Spark of
  Resistance's "3+ enemies within 15 m" is treated as always holding (D7).
- **Counting, next** (D33): only Armor Charge is counted. The decay (one stack every 10 s, slower with Time Dilation) is
  shown, never applied, so a loop that waits between gaining and spending holds fewer than it says. Elemental Charge's
  activation progress stays a chance (every 2nd Ionic Trace with two copies); Emergency Reinforcement's fourth duration
  (15 s, unlabelled in the Compendium) is `?` at x6. The next task (agreed in #70, which counted Armor Charge):
  **Count Bolt Charge, Combination Blow and Slice through the loop.** They stay declared today: the player says
  `max:bolt-charge`, `max:combination-blow`, `max:slice` when a threshold is reached (D3). Count them as #70 counts Armor
  Charge (D33): mark them `counted: true` in the glossary, keep a tally of sure stacks (rules that always fire) and
  chance stacks (chance rules and what they set off), capped at the glossary's `maxStacks` (a grant at the cap shows as
  wasted, sure stacks at the cap put the buff at its max), with each gain and spend listed under the state's count. The
  count decides: a rule that needs the max (Shinobu's Vow and Flashover at x10 Bolt Charge, Combination Blow's x3, Slice
  ending after its 5 hits) fires for sure when the sure stacks reach it, as a chance when only the chance ones do, and
  not otherwise; a spend takes stacks off (sure first), and `max:` stays offered while the sure stacks are below the cap.
  Check each gain and spend against the Compendium (Bolt Charge's discharge resets it, Slice gains one per severing hit
  and ends at its 5th) and the goldens of the creator's loop, which declare these maxes today.
- **Kill counts, next**: the enemy-count selector names the counts authored rules need (the Siphons, Spark of
  Amplitude, Spark of Discharge, Synergy, and the weapon perks One for All, Stats for All, Reaper's Tithe, Hatchling,
  Veteran's Wisdom, Unrelenting and Radiolaria Transposer). Not authored yet: Multikill Clip and Desperado (their kills count at a reload: the
  Reload step, "Weapon perks" task 1), and the perks that need enemies nearby rather than in one action (Surrounded,
  Cornered, Danger Zone, Threat Detector, Extrovert). A precision hit isn't a step, so Hatchling's and Veteran's Wisdom's
  "or a precision kill" is the chance on fewer than 3 kills; a strike by this weapon (Veteran's Wisdom's shockwave,
  Radiolaria Transposer's explosion and pool) hits one enemy, and its damage is a fact in the perk's description.
  The next task (#79 follow-up):
  **Add the weapon perks that need enemies nearby (Surrounded, Cornered, Danger Zone, Threat Detector, Extrovert).**
  "Nearby" isn't one action's hits or kills, so the player declares how many enemies are near (D3, D4), the way the
  enemy-count selector declares an action's count, and each perk names the count it needs there; each with its
  enhanced trait, `weapon:this`, and the Compendium's numbers (`"?"` until known).
- **Cooldowns, next**: a rule fires on every event of a step, so a source with a cooldown fires once per kill of one
  action when the game fires it once. The next task (raised in #70):
  **Fire a rule with a cooldown at most once per action.** Within one step (one action's hits and kills), a rule whose
  source has a cooldown fires once, and the step says so ("once: 6 s cooldown"); later steps aren't limited, as time
  isn't modelled (D1, D5). The sources: Photonic Flare (~6 s), Firepower, Heavy Handed, the 7 s energy mods, Spark of
  Ions (10 s between traces), Impact Induction, and the elemental pickups — one Firesprite (Solar#5) or Void Breach
  (Void#7), 5 s each, and one Tangle (Strand#4, 12 s) per step instead of one per kill. Ionic Traces have no shared cooldown
  (Arc#6): Shock and Clear and Spark of Discharge keep making one per jolted kill (an r/destiny2builds thread confirms
  it for Luminopotent's 4-piece; `builds/skip-grenade-hunter/discrepancies.md` has the sources). The cooldown is a fact
  of the rule (a new key, e.g. `cooldown: 6s`), shown with its outcome; check every value against the snapshot.
- **Armor picker, next**: the mods of a picked piece aren't read (the build keeps the loadout's mods); armor of
  another class's character isn't offered; an icon bungie.net doesn't serve shows broken.
- **Exotic class items' spirits**: Relativism, Stoicism and Solipsism are listed in the armor picker's Every item, but
  their two random perks (spirits) can't be picked and no rule authors them: the build wears the item without them.
- **Every item, next**: the list (~7,500 items, ~5 MB of JSON parsed in .NET at startup: its second step holds the
  page's thread for ~5 s on a desktop) and its grid (up to ~930 squares for energy weapons) could be lighter on a phone
  (fewer fields, the items read in batches, or a grid that draws only the rows in view).
